# Macro event calendar 2026: sources and checks

Window: 1 Jan to 31 Oct 2026. Verified on 14 Oct 2026. All times US Eastern.

## Official sources used
- BLS archived news releases (embargo line read on each): https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_MMDDYYYY.htm and cpi_MMDDYYYY.htm for every past NFP and CPI release (Jan to Oct NFP, Jan to Sep CPI). All state 8:30 a.m. ET and match the dates in the CSV.
- BLS release lists: https://www.bls.gov/bls/news-release/empsit.htm and cpi.htm (archive file names), and monthly calendar pages https://www.bls.gov/schedule/2026/MM_sched.htm (Jan, Feb, Oct read).
- Federal Reserve: https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm (meeting dates), each statement page monetary2026MMDDa.htm (2:00 p.m. ET), and the 2026 FOMC press release list https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/2026-press-fomc.htm.
- BEA: https://www.bea.gov/news/schedule/full (schedule) and each Personal Income and Outlays news release page under https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/ (embargo line read on each).

## Rescheduled releases
- BLS Jan and Feb 2026 calendar pages carry the notice that, due to the lapse in government services, release dates are subject to change. The CSV uses the dates on which the releases were actually issued (confirmed from the embargo lines): NFP 9 Jan and 11 Feb, CPI 13 Jan and 13 Feb. The original planned dates were not retrievable from the pages read.
- PCE 22 Jan 2026 was a combined Oct and Nov 2025 release at 10:00 ET (catch-up after the 2025 shutdown). The Feb, Mar and Apr 2026 PCE dates (20 Feb, 13 Mar, 9 Apr) are later than the usual end-of-month pattern; the BEA schedule page contains "New Date" label configuration but its row mapping could not be read from the page text, so the original dates are not stated.
